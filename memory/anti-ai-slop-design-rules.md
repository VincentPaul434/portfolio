When doing any design/UI work for this user, avoid the patterns that read as "AI slop":
excessive centered layouts, purple gradients, uniform rounded corners (vary radius by
element type instead), and the Inter font.

**Why:** The user is actively trying to make work (e.g. their [[portfolio]]) look human-designed,
not generated. These four are the most recognizable tells of model-default design output.

**How to apply:** Prefer left-aligned/asymmetric layouts; pick a non-default accent (not
purple/indigo/violet); give containers vs. interactive controls different corner radii rather
than one global radius; choose a distinctive typeface (e.g. IBM Plex, Fraunces) over Inter.
